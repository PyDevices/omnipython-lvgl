# Changelog

One release covers the generator, the three ports and the `pydevices-lvgl`
wheel, and its notes live with the release:
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/PyDevices/omnipython-lvgl/releases). The
version is the LVGL line plus a counter, such as `9.5.53`. The firmware ports
are built into your firmware from a commit, so a firmware's
`lvgl_micropython.__revision__` names the commit it came from.

Releases up to `9.5.52` were made before the bindings and the three ports
shared one repository: the wheel's are in
[lvgl-python's releases](https://github.com/PyDevices/lvgl-python/releases), and
the bindings' in [lvgl-bindings' tags](https://github.com/PyDevices/lvgl-bindings/tags).

What's planned next is in the [roadmap](https://github.com/PyDevices/omnipython-lvgl/blob/main/ROADMAP.md).
